Common questions

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. I have delivered work for clients in India, the UK, Australia and Indonesia, through Upwork and directly. Nepal time (UTC+5:45) overlaps well with UK and European mornings and Asia-Pacific afternoons.

How quickly do you reply?

Within 24 hours for email and the contact form. WhatsApp is usually faster during Nepal business hours.

Can you take over a website or system someone else built?

Usually, yes. I start with a short review of the code, hosting, DNS and access, then tell you what is safe to keep and what needs fixing before anything changes.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, as a monthly arrangement for IT support, hosting and maintenance, or CRM administration. Scope and response times are agreed in writing.

Can you move my site to AWS or a new host?

Yes. That includes DNS, SSL, email records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), backups and a rollback plan, so visitors don't notice the switch.

Are you available for full-time roles?

I'm open to remote roles in IT management, platform or full-stack engineering. My CV and LinkedIn have the details; email is the best first step.
